Map calibration

How to calibrate (once)
  1. Open the in-game map at the zoom you normally use, then press your calibrate hotkey.
  2. Left-click the tip of your character's arrow on the map. Press Enter to save and go back to the game.
  3. Fly at least 50 m (diagonally if you can), reopen the map, press the hotkey again, click your arrow again, Enter.

After two clicks the markers line up; a third click from somewhere else also corrects a rotated map. If your map stays centred on you, the overlay switches to follow mode by itself: scroll the wheel until a cube marker sits on its cube. While calibrating: right-drag or arrow keys move the layer, wheel scales (Shift = fine), R rotates 90°, F mirrors, M switches fixed/follow, Backspace clears the clicks. Calibration matches one map zoom level.
